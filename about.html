What Eventify does

From "what's on" to "I'm in", in four moves.

🔎

Discover

Search, filter by city and category, sort by what matters — soonest, cheapest, or most talked-about.

🎟

Book or RSVP

Paid events get General and VIP tiers with a running total. Free events skip straight to a one-tap RSVP.

📡

Watch it live

Seats and attendee counts move while you browse, with a heads-up the moment something is close to selling out.

📣

Host

Sign in, fill in the details with a live preview of your ticket stub, and publish straight into the Discover feed.

How we build

A few things we don't compromise on.

01

Design with intention

No default dashboard template. The ticket-stub, the barcode, the perforated edge — every event on the platform looks like it belongs to the same show.

02

Nothing to set up

Eventify runs entirely in your browser. There's no account wall between you and browsing, and no backend to provision before you can try it.

03

Live should feel live

A seat counter that never moves isn't live, it's decoration. Ours updates while you watch, and tells you when it matters.

04

Free is a first-class flow

Free events aren't a paid flow with the price hidden. They get their own simple, one-step RSVP.
